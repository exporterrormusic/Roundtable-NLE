Corrected structure:
  • Left panel (between rail & table) hosts NEW / OPEN / SETTINGS — slides in from the left like today, with a close ✕. Try the rail buttons or the "Left:" switcher.
  • Right pane is details only: Inspector when a project is selected, Empty when none, Batch when multiple. Expands from the right; collapsible via ⟩.

  • Orientation-aware thumbnails, current-project row stripe, and the keyboard hint strip carry over.
