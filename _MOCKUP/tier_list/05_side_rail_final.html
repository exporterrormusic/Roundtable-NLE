Tier List panel — chosen (Side Rail + Pool Bin).
  • Pool = a Project-Bin-style browser: search, subbins, and list ⇄ icon views (reuses the app's bin widgets); built for dozens of entries.
  • Events: drag an entry onto a tier row → a POPUP + DROP pair, each a one-line row. Start/end are clickable time buttons (⤓/⤒) that stamp the playhead; the DROP row also has an inline tier ▾ to change the rank without re-dragging (POPUP shows “—”, no rank).
  • Board layout rules (from the original): a top band is reserved for your channel banner (twitter/youtube handles); the spotlight is centered on screen (dashed guide); entries never cross into the reserved commentator zone on the right (hatched); and a tier wraps to a new line & grows taller when it fills (tier C).
  • Grid button opens tier/colour/title config; the Entry-shape selector is the per-list aspect (Banner / Square / Vertical 9:16 / Custom).
  • Commentators are out of scope here — they'll be their own separate timeline panel (the greyed “Commentators (soon)” dock tab), designed later.
