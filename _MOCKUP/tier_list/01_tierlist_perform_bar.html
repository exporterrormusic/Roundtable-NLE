Layout 1 — Perform Bar.
  • The middle bar is the whole point: Spotlight (Q) then a colored tier key (1–6), each press stamping the current playhead. No timecodes typed.
  • Entry pool shows what's already placed (dim + tier badge) vs still in pool; the blue NEXT card is what Spotlight will grab. ＋ Drop PNGs adds entries.
  • The event track renders every stamp as a draggable marker (white = spotlight, colored = drop / reorder); drag to retime, right-click to delete.
  • Commentators view: two 2×4 banks of drop .png boxes; the speaker is highlighted, the rest dimmed. Left bank greys out while a tier list is on screen.
