ROUNDTABLE · Commentators — Full-canvas states

How the banks compose against real program content — 1920×1080 shown at 0.5× mockup 02 / 02

Three static states of the Commentators overlay on a full 16:9 canvas. All geometry is measured from the reference frame: top banner band reserves 5.65% of height (61px), each bank occupies the outer 13% of width (250px) from y=61 to the bottom, cells are near-square (≈247×255) with ~4px gaps, on pure black. Highlight shown is the recommended default (Variant A edge accent from mockup 01). Speakers are picked statically per state.

State 2

Tier List active — left bank auto-hidden

State 3

Dense — 8 per side (2×4)

With 5–8 boxes on a side, the bank switches to two columns of four inside the same 13% strip: cells halve in width (≈123×255 real) and portraits crop tighter on the face. NEON (right bank) is speaking.
