★ CURRENT DIRECTION — Simple + Scenes (06)

Drag PNGs onto LEFT / RIGHT — no framing controls, boxes always the same fixed size, stacks center vertically by count (5+ wraps to a second full-size column inward). SCENE dividers in the speaker list change the cast: a new scene copies the previous cast, you add/remove, and at the divider characters slide in / slide out / glide to their new spots (the preview animates it). SIDES edits the scene under the playhead; the SPEAKERS list auto-populates from the audio tracks and follows the playhead; override = a per-row Auto ▾ combo.
