Speaker-highlight treatment for the stacked portrait banks — 3 variants mockup 01 / 02

A Commentators clip renders up to 8 still PNG portraits per side in a 13%-wide strip on pure black, below the top banner band. The box whose label matches the character-named audio track that has a clip under the playhead is the speaker: full brightness plus an accent treatment; everyone else is dimmed to ~55%. The three variants below differ only in what the accent treatment looks like.

Each bank below is the right-side 4-up column (real cells ≈ 247×255 @1080p) shown at ~0.55 scale. The speaker cycles automatically so the transition can be compared across all three at once.

Hover a bank to pause the cycle
Variant A

Edge accent (reference-faithful)

No frames, no labels. The speaker is full brightness with a 4px accent bar hugging the cell's inner edge (the edge facing the program video); everyone else dims. This is the closest match to the treatment in the existing videos.

Recommendation: ship Variant A (edge accent) as the default — it is what the existing uploads already look like, keeps portraits full-bleed, and is the cheapest to render. Offer Variant B's name labels as a per-clip toggle (label strip + accent tag) for videos with guest commentators; skip C's scale animation for v1 since portraits are intentionally still images.
