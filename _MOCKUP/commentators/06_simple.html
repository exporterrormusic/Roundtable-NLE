Drag PNGs onto a side; cut the video into SCENES to change who's on screen. The SIDES editor always edits the scene under the playhead. + Scene drops a divider into the speaker list at the playhead — the new scene starts as a copy of the previous cast, then you add (drag in) or remove (✕) characters. When playback crosses a divider, portraits slide in from their side, slide out, or glide to their new centered spots — watch the preview below, it cycles through three scenes.

Fills itself from your character audio tracks. + Scene inserts a divider at the playhead. Deleting a divider (✕) merges it into the previous scene. Click a row's Auto ▾ to force a different speaker — or nobody — for that segment.

Speaker highlight: Variant A with no accent bar — the speaker is simply full brightness, everyone else dimmed. Scene transitions: characters kept across a divider glide to their new centered position; joiners slide in from their side's edge (sliding behind anyone already on screen); leavers slide out the same way.
