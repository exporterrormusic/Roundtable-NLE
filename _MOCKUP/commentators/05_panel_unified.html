Layout C vs A / B — Pros: the most compact of the three; the whole feature (preview, both banks, inspector, overrides) is visible at once with zero collapsing or tab-switching, so it survives the short heights timeline panels usually get. Cons: small targets — bank cells and override rows are dense, and the inspector gets cramped on narrow docks. Compare: Layout A — Bank Editor (preview-centered, banks flanking, roomier) · Layout B — Side Rail (mirrors the Tier List panel, taller).
